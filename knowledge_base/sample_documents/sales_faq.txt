FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
----------------------------

PRICING & BILLING
------------------

Q: Can I try NovaTech before buying?
A: Yes! All products come with a 14-day free trial. No credit card required. You get full access to all features during the trial.

Q: What happens after the trial?
A: You can choose a plan that fits your needs. If you don't upgrade, your account is paused (data preserved for 30 days).

Q: Do you offer discounts for annual billing?
A: Yes, annual billing saves you 20% compared to monthly. Enterprise customers can negotiate custom multi-year agreements.

Q: Is there a minimum contract period?
A: Monthly plans have no minimum. Annual plans require a 12-month commitment.

Q: What payment methods do you accept?
A: Credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), ACH bank transfer, and Purchase Orders for Enterprise clients.

Q: Do you offer non-profit discounts?
A: Yes, we offer 40% discount for registered non-profits. Contact sales with your 501(c)3 documentation.

IMPLEMENTATION & ONBOARDING
-----------------------------

Q: How long does implementation take?
A: Standard onboarding takes 2-4 weeks. Complex enterprise implementations with custom integrations may take 6-12 weeks.

Q: Do you help migrate our existing data?
A: Yes, free data migration is included for plans over $500/month. We support imports from Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho, and CSV.

Q: What training do you provide?
A: Every plan includes video training library access. Pro and Enterprise include live training sessions and dedicated success managers.

Q: Can we pilot with one team first?
A: Absolutely. We encourage departmental pilots. Many customers start with one team and expand company-wide after proving ROI.

TECHNICAL QUESTIONS
--------------------

Q: Where is our data stored?
A: Data is stored in AWS/GCP data centers. US customers use US-East/West. EU customers use EU-West (GDPR compliant). We offer data residency options for Enterprise.

Q: What integrations do you support?
A: We have 300+ pre-built integrations including Salesforce, HubSpot, Microsoft Teams, Slack, Zoom, Gmail, Outlook, Zapier, and a full REST API for custom integrations.

Q: Is NovaTech SOC2 certified?
A: Yes, we are SOC2 Type II certified, ISO 27001 certified, and GDPR compliant. We can provide security documentation upon request.

Q: What is your uptime SLA?
A: We guarantee 99.9% uptime on all plans. Enterprise plans include 99.95% SLA with financial penalties for downtime.

Q: Does NovaTech offer on-premise deployment?
A: We offer private cloud deployment for Enterprise customers with strict data sovereignty requirements. Contact sales for pricing.

COMPETITIVE COMPARISON
-----------------------

Q: How does NovaCRM compare to Salesforce?
A: NovaCRM Pro offers similar features at 60% lower cost with AI built-in from day one. Salesforce requires expensive add-ons for AI features. NovaCRM is easier to configure and has better mobile apps.

Q: Why choose NovaTech over HubSpot?
A: HubSpot is excellent for SMBs but becomes expensive at scale. NovaTech provides enterprise-grade features, better API, and AI-native architecture at competitive pricing.

SECURITY & COMPLIANCE
----------------------

A: Yes, we offer HIPAA-compliant deployment for healthcare customers. BAA is available. Contact legal@novatech-solutions.com.

CANCELLATION & REFUNDS
-----------------------

Q: Can I cancel anytime?
A: Monthly plans can be cancelled anytime with 30 days notice. Annual plans can be cancelled with a pro-rated refund within 30 days of purchase.

Q: What happens to my data if I cancel?
A: Data is available for export for 60 days after cancellation. After 60 days, data is securely deleted.
